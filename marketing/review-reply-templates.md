# Google Review Reply Templates — Space City Bites

Quick-reference replies for Google Business Profile reviews. **Rotate through the options**
so no two consecutive replies are identical (Google's spam filters and readers both dislike
copy-paste). Personalize with the reviewer's **name** whenever it's available — it lifts the
reply out of "generic" and Google rewards personalization. **One emoji max** keeps it warm
without looking spammy.

---

## ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ 5-Star — no or minimal text

Use for ratings-only reviews or ones with a word or two ("Great!", "Best wings").

1. Thank you for the 5 stars! 🚀 We appreciate you and can't wait to feed you again. — Space City Bites
2. Appreciate the love! 🙌 Thanks for choosing Space City Bites — see you next time for more halal bites.
3. 5 stars means the world to us — thank you! Come back hungry. 🍗 — The Space City Bites team
4. Thanks so much for the rating! We're glad you enjoyed it. Big cravings, bigger bites — we'll be here. 🔥
5. Thank you! ⭐️ Reviews like this keep us going. Can't wait to see you again at Space City Bites.

## ⭐️⭐️ / ⭐️ 1–2 Star — critical

Stay calm, professional, and take it offline. Never argue, never blame the customer, never
get defensive in public. Apologize, own it, and give a direct contact. Keep it short.

**General:**
> We're sorry this didn't meet your expectations, {Name} — that's not the experience we want
> anyone to have. We'd really like to make it right. Please reach out to us at (713) 621-3128
> so we can look into it. — Space City Bites

**Food/quality issue:**
> Thank you for letting us know, {Name}. This isn't the standard we hold ourselves to, and we'd
> like the chance to fix it. Please give us a call at (713) 621-3128 — we want to make it right.

**Wait time / service:**
> We're sorry about the wait, {Name} — we know your time matters. We're working on it, and we'd
> love a chance to give you a better experience. Reach out at (713) 621-3128 and let's make it up to you.

**Suspected fake / never-a-customer review** (be careful, stay polite — you can also flag it to
Google for removal):
> We can't find a record of your visit and want to make sure this review is meant for us. If you
> did dine with us and had a bad experience, please reach out at (713) 621-3128 — we'd genuinely
> like to help.

## Rules of thumb

- **Respond to every review** — 5-star included. It signals an engaged owner and boosts local SEO.
- **Speed matters:** aim to reply within 24–48 hours.
- **Names > generic.** Use the reviewer's first name whenever it's shown.
- **Never reveal private order details** in a public reply.
- **Take negatives offline** with a phone number/email; don't litigate in the thread.
- **Rotate templates** — don't paste the same words twice in a row.
